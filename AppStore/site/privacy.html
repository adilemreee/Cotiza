Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Precia does not sell or share personal data, and has no ads, analytics or user accounts. Your jobs, clients and prices stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The one exception is a signing link you choose to send: that estimate is kept on our server only until your client signs it (section 8).

1. Who we are

Precia is made by Adil Emre Karayürek, an individual developer in Türkiye.
Contact: destek@adilemree.xyz

2. Data stored on your iPhone

The following is kept only in the app's storage on your device, to make the app work:

  • Your business details and, if you add one, your logo
  • Clients you add: name, phone, email and job address
  • Estimates, invoices, line items (including lines added from a receipt), notes, dates and saved prices
  • Signatures your clients draw on your phone, with the name they enter and the date
  • App settings

This data never reaches us. If you're signed in to iCloud, it is also kept in your private iCloud database so it appears on your other iPhones and iPads with the same Apple Account. That database belongs to your Apple Account; we cannot read it. Data is also included in your device backups according to your own settings.

3. Voice input

When you tap the microphone, Precia uses Apple's Speech framework to turn what you say into text. When your iPhone supports on-device recognition for your language, this happens entirely on the device. Otherwise Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. Precia does not record, store or send your audio.

Microphone and speech recognition permission is asked only when you first tap the microphone. You can always type the job instead.

4. How the estimate is written

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the estimate is written by Apple's on-device language model. On other iPhones Precia uses a built-in parser. In both cases your job description stays on the device and is not sent to us.

5. Photos and camera

If you choose a logo, only the image you pick is copied into the app.

To scan a store receipt, you take a photo with the camera or pick one. Precia reads the text on your iPhone to find items and prices. The photo itself is not saved: only the lines you choose to add are kept. Camera permission is asked only the first time you scan with the camera. Precia does not read your photo library.

6. Contacts

When you tap “Choose from Contacts”, iOS shows its own contact picker. Precia receives only the contact you pick and copies its name, phone, email and address into that client. It does not get access to your other contacts and does not ask for Contacts permission.

7. Sending documents

When you send an estimate or invoice by text message, Precia fills in your client's number, a short note and the PDF, and you review and send it in Messages. For WhatsApp, email or other apps, you choose the app and the recipient in the iOS share sheet. Precia never sends anything by itself; from then on the app you choose handles it.

8. Signing links

When you tap “Send a signing link”, Precia uploads that one estimate to our server at cotiza.adilemree.xyz so your client can open it in a browser: the estimate's PDF and what the page shows (your business name and contact details, the client's name, the items, prices and terms). When your client signs, the server also stores the name they type, their signature, the time, and their IP address and browser, as a record of the approval.

The link is private: it is a long random address, it is not indexed by search engines, and only the device that created it can read the result. As soon as Precia collects the signature, it deletes the request from the server. Anything not collected is deleted automatically 30 days after it was sent or signed. “Cancel the link” deletes it right away. These records are not backed up, sold or shared, and are used only to show the estimate and return the signature to you.

9. Reminders

If you allow notifications, Precia schedules reminders on your iPhone: when an estimate has had no answer for a few days and when an invoice is past due. They are created on the device; there is no notification server. You can turn them off in Precia's Settings or in iOS Settings.

10. Widgets, Siri and Spotlight

The Precia widget shows the totals the app saves in a space shared only with its own widget on your iPhone. Your estimates and invoices are added to your iPhone's search index so you can find them in Spotlight and ask Siri about them. That index stays on your device and is managed by iOS.

11. Purchases

Precia Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

12. Analytics, ads and third parties

Precia contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. No usage data is collected and nothing is shared with or sold to third parties.

13. Deleting your data

  • Delete any estimate, invoice, client or saved price inside the app. Deleting a document, or tapping “Remove signature”, also deletes its signature.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device, including what the widget shows and what Spotlight finds. A copy stays in your private iCloud until you delete it in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage storage › Precia.
  • We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

14. Children

Precia is a business tool, is not directed at children and is rated 4+ on the App Store.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the CCPA in California, the GDPR in Europe or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete your personal data. Your data is on your device and in your iCloud, so deleting it there is enough; signing-link records delete themselves as described in section 8. You can still contact us at destek@adilemree.xyz; we reply within 30 days.

16. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
