Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Write to us.

The microphone doesn't work

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Apps › Cotiza and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. You can also type the job instead.

A price or quantity came out wrong

Tap the line in the estimate to change its description, quantity, unit or price. Lines Cotiza couldn't price are marked “Add price”, and Cotiza never makes up a price you didn't say.

How do I send the estimate to my client?

Tap “Preview and send”. “Text message” opens Messages with your client's number, a short note in their language and the PDF. “WhatsApp” hands the PDF and the note to WhatsApp, where you pick the chat. “Email or other apps” opens the share sheet.

How does my client sign the estimate?

Open the estimate (Cotiza Pro). “Get client signature”: hand your client the phone and they sign with a finger. “Send a signing link”: send a link by text or WhatsApp; your client opens it, reviews the estimate and signs in the browser, and you get a notification. Either way the estimate is marked approved and the signature is printed on the PDF with the date.

How do I add materials from a store receipt?

In an estimate or invoice, tap “Scan a receipt”, then take a photo or choose one. Check the items and prices Cotiza found, untick any you don't want and tap Add. Totals, tax and payment lines are left out.

Can the estimate be in English if I speak Spanish?

Yes. Set the document language in Settings, or per estimate. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the items are written in that language; on others, Cotiza translates them on the device when the language pack is available.

What do I get with Cotiza Pro?

Unlimited estimates, invoices, recurring invoices, client signatures, profit reports, your logo on every document and no “Made with Cotiza” footer. The free plan includes 3 estimates.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name › Subscriptions › Cotiza Pro. Or in Cotiza, open Settings › Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

I got a new phone and Pro is gone

In Cotiza, tap Go Pro › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Where is my data? How do I move it to a new phone?

On your iPhone and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud. Sign in to a new iPhone or an iPad with the same Apple Account and your jobs appear there. See the privacy policy.

How do I give my accountant my numbers?

Tap the chart button on the home screen (Cotiza Pro). Pick a month or a year and tap Export for a spreadsheet (CSV) or a PDF summary with revenue, sales tax, materials cost and profit.

How do recurring invoices work?

Open an invoice and set Repeat to every week, every 2 weeks or every month. On that day Cotiza makes a copy with a new number and the same items, and reminds you to send it.
